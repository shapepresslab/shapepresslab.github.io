# Shapepress

A browser-based poster generator. Pick a style, adjust the settings, and download your poster as a high-resolution PNG or a scalable SVG.

## Styles

- **Bauhaus Grid** — up to 20 columns of tiles drawn from 32 shapes: circles, arches, stripes, eyes, stars, moons, flowers, chevrons, waves, stairs and more
- **Line Waves** — concentric lines that snake, loop or form a maze
- **Color Blend** — stacked shapes in smooth gradients (ribbon, tunnel, fan, swirl)
- **Stripe Rhythm** — bars, pills and waves
- **Op Art** — stripes that bend around hidden forms

- **Confetti** — scattered Memphis-style outline or solid shapes, with an optional hidden word
- **Micro Geometry** — 65 technical line-art modules (spirographs, Lissajous curves, moiré, wire cubes, truchet tiles and more) on a grid of up to 20 columns, with a vortex, tunnel or mesh feature shape
- **Interface** — sci-fi dashboard graphics from 112 elements (dials, radar, gauges, charts, heat maps, waveforms, keypads, terminals, windows, icons, wireframes and more) with mono labels, up to 20 columns
- **Micro Life** — 60 playful creatures: cells, microbes, viruses, jellyfish, starfish, coral, leaves, mushrooms and more, with an optional hidden word

- **Micro Characters** — original doodle characters built from 30 body shapes, 31 eye styles, 28 mouths, 30 hats and head extras, 10 accessories, 12 body patterns and 10 emotion marks, in 13 moods and four layouts (sticker grid, crowd, hero, parade)

- **Cyberpunk** — angled circuit bars that bend together at 45°, circuit traces with nodes, chamfered frames, hazard stripes and HUD marks (58 elements), with direction, mirror and an optional label plate
- **Architecture** — 48 building elements (lighthouses, pagodas, brutalist slabs, bridges, geodesic domes, iso blocks and more) in building icons, street scenes, waterfronts, isometric cities, facades, blueprints, patchwork, tilted skylines or floor plans
- **Minimalism** — 42 line glyphs and 21 layouts: balance, odd one out, line field, nested squares, duality, one line, long shadows, halftone, gesture, stack, rays, overlap, sequences, horizons, Swiss blocks, star patterns, cropped letters and more
- **Repeat** — seamless patterns in five families (28 motif repeats including leaves, pine trees, lotus, scallop shells, pinwheels, baubles, keyhole arches, raindrops, mushrooms and lightning bolts; geometric tiles, ornament, textiles, tossed prints) on grid, brick, half-drop, hex or diamond lattices, reusing Bauhaus, Confetti and Minimalism elements, with 22 textiles including holiday knit, snowflakes, candy stripe, harlequin, diamond trellis, stepped ikat diamonds, paisley, block-print stars, tie-dye dots, rangoli, jaali and running stitch
- **Mix & Match** — combines the other styles in clean, separate panels (mosaic, feature, columns, rows, grid or porthole layouts), with shared colors, panel grounds and per-panel settings that adapt to each panel's size

## Features

- Live preview with one-tap variations, "New layout" and "Surprise me"
- 32 color palettes in five groups (Classic, Black & white, Shades, Vivid, Dark), a "Shades of any color" palette builder, plus editable colors, background and ink
- Poster formats: 2:3, A-series, 50 × 70 cm, 3:4, 4:5, square, story 9:16, landscape 3:2, wide 16:9 and ultrawide 21:9
- Margin, color panel, paper grain, 16 textures (dots, halftone, grid, graph paper, lines, scanlines, diagonals, crosshatch, isometric, waves and more) with size, strength, color and placement controls, and poster text (title, subtitle, number, corner note)
- Undo / redo, keyboard shortcuts (R = new layout, S = surprise me)
- Export PNG up to 12,000 px wide, or SVG vector files
- Settings are remembered in your browser

## Typefaces

The display typefaces are open-source fonts from Google Fonts (via Fontsource), licensed under the SIL Open Font License: Anton, Bebas Neue, Big Shoulders Display, Archivo Black, Bricolage Grotesque, Syne, Unbounded, Dela Gothic One, Rubik Mono One, Space Mono, Bungee, Monoton, Abril Fatface, DM Serif Display, Fraunces, Shrikhand and Pacifico. They are embedded in the page, and text is exported as vector outlines, so downloads look the same on every computer.

## Running it

The published `index.html` is a compiled build: fonts are embedded and the app code is minified. Open it in a browser, or host it anywhere that serves static files.
